## Analytical Framework

1. **Supply / demand balance** — production data, inventory levels (EIA, LME, CFTC COT report).
2. **Cost of production** — marginal cost curve determines floor price.
3. **Macro sensitivity** — dollar strength, China PMI, global growth.
4. **Geopolitical risk premium** — supply disruption probabilities.
5. **Positioning** — CFTC Commitments of Traders (COT); extreme speculative positioning is a contrarian signal.

## Key Data Sources

- **EIA (Energy Information Administration)** — crude, natgas, petroleum product inventories and production.
- **CFTC COT Report** — speculative and commercial positioning (weekly, released Fridays).
- **LME / CME** — metals and energy futures data.
- **World Gold Council** — gold ETF flows, central bank demand.
- **USDA WASDE** — agricultural supply/demand estimates.

## Important Indicators

- **WTI / Brent spread** — domestic vs. international crude benchmark.
- **Copper/Gold ratio** — proxy for global growth expectations. Rising = growth optimism.
- **Backwardation / Contango** — backwardation (spot > futures) signals tight physical supply.
- **DXY** — dollar strength typically weighs on USD-denominated commodity prices.
- **China Caixin PMI** — China's industrial activity is the marginal demand driver for most base metals.

## Current Themes to Watch

- Energy transition: copper demand for electrification vs. near-term supply constraints.
- Gold: central bank buying trend, real yield trajectory.
- Oil: OPEC+ discipline vs. US shale production growth.
- Agricultural: La Niña / El Niño crop impacts, Black Sea grain corridor.
