## Analytical Framework

1. **Business quality** — moat, pricing power, management track record.
2. **Growth** — revenue CAGR, TAM headroom, unit economics.
3. **Valuation** — P/E, EV/EBITDA, PEG vs. sector peers and history.
4. **Catalysts** — upcoming earnings, product launches, macro shifts that could re-rate the stock.
5. **Risk** — key person, regulatory, competitive, macro sensitivity.

## Key Data Sources

- **SEC EDGAR** — 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K filings (primary source).
- **Earnings call transcripts** — Seeking Alpha, Motley Fool, company IR pages.
- **Consensus estimates** — Bloomberg, Visible Alpha, LSEG (formerly Refinitiv).
- **Alternative data** — app downloads (Sensor Tower), web traffic (Similarweb), job postings.

## Important Indicators

- S&P 500 forward P/E (current vs. 10-yr average ~17×).
- VIX — fear gauge; spikes above 30 signal stress.
- Earnings revision breadth — more upgrades than downgrades is bullish.
- High-yield credit spreads — widening signals risk-off before equities often react.

## Current Themes to Watch

- AI infrastructure buildout (capex cycle for hyperscalers).
- Reshoring and supply-chain diversification (India, Mexico).
- Consumer resilience vs. credit stress at lower income tiers.
