## Analytical Framework

1. **On-chain fundamentals** — active addresses, transaction volume, TVL, fee revenue.
2. **Supply dynamics** — issuance schedule, unlock schedule, exchange balances.
3. **Narrative / cycle position** — where are we in the halving cycle? What's the dominant narrative?
4. **Developer activity** — GitHub commits, protocol upgrades, ecosystem growth.
5. **Macro overlay** — risk appetite, real yields, dollar strength.

## Key Data Sources

- **Glassnode** — on-chain data (BTC, ETH); miner metrics, exchange flows.
- **DeFiLlama** — TVL across protocols and chains.
- **Dune Analytics** — custom on-chain queries.
- **CoinGecko / CoinMarketCap** — price, volume, market cap.
- **Messari** — protocol research and fundamentals.
- **The Block** — institutional and regulatory news.

## Important Indicators

- BTC exchange balance — declining = supply leaving exchanges (bullish).
- Funding rates — positive = longs paying shorts; extreme positive = overheated.
- ETH burn rate (EIP-1559) vs. issuance — net deflationary above ~16 gwei base fee.
- Stablecoin market cap — growth signals fresh capital entering the ecosystem.

## Current Themes to Watch

- Bitcoin ETF inflow/outflow dynamics post-approval.
- Ethereum L2 ecosystem maturity (TVL, fee revenue to L1).
- Regulatory clarity in the US (SEC vs. CFTC jurisdiction).
- Solana as an Ethereum competitor for retail and DeFi.
