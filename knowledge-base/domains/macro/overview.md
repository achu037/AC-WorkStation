## Analytical Framework

1. **Monetary policy** — Fed funds rate trajectory, QT/QE, FOMC dot plot vs. market pricing.
2. **Inflation** — CPI/PCE trends, core vs. headline, shelter lag, wage growth.
3. **Growth** — GDP, PMI, ISM, labour market (NFP, jobless claims, JOLTS).
4. **Liquidity** — global M2, Fed balance sheet, RRP drain, TGA drawdown.
5. **Credit** — high-yield spreads, bank lending standards, mortgage rates.

## Key Data Sources

- **FRED (Federal Reserve)** — all US macro data, free, comprehensive.
- **BLS** — CPI, PPI, NFP releases.
- **CME FedWatch** — market-implied Fed rate expectations.
- **US Treasury** — yield curve data, TGA balance.
- **BIS / IMF / World Bank** — global macro and financial stability reports.

## Important Indicators

- **2s10s spread** — yield curve shape; inversion often precedes recession.
- **Real Fed Funds Rate** — nominal rate minus CPI. Restrictive when positive and high.
- **Global M2** — loose correlation with risk asset prices with ~3 month lead.
- **ISM Manufacturing PMI** — below 50 = contraction. Key for industrial/commodity positioning.
- **JOLTS quits rate** — high quits = labour market confidence; decline = softening.

## Current Themes to Watch

- Timing and pace of Fed rate cuts.
- Fiscal deficit sustainability (US debt/GDP trajectory).
- China stimulus efficacy and property sector resolution.
- Geopolitical supply shocks (energy, semiconductors).
