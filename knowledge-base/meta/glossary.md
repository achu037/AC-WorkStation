## Markets & Portfolio

**Alpha** — Return in excess of a benchmark after adjusting for risk. The part of return attributable to skill rather than market exposure.

**Beta** — Sensitivity of an asset's return to a benchmark (usually the broader market). Beta > 1 means the asset amplifies market moves.

**Sharpe Ratio** — Risk-adjusted return: `(Return − Risk-Free Rate) / Std Dev`. Higher is better. Below 1 is generally poor.

**Drawdown** — Peak-to-trough decline in portfolio or asset value. Max drawdown is the largest such decline over a period.

**Conviction** — Confidence in a thesis, rated 1–10. 1 = very speculative / watching only. 7+ = willing to size meaningfully.

**Timeframe** — Expected holding period for a thesis: *near-term* (< 3 months), *medium-term* (3–12 months), *long-term* (> 1 year).

**Long / Short** — Long = expecting price to rise. Short = expecting price to fall (often via borrowing and selling).

## Macro

**Yield Curve** — Graph of bond yields across maturities. Normal = upward sloping. Inverted (short rates > long rates) historically precedes recessions.

**Real Yield** — Nominal bond yield minus inflation expectations. Positive real yields raise the hurdle rate for risk assets.

**DXY** — US Dollar Index measuring the dollar against a basket of six major currencies. Rising DXY often pressures risk assets and commodities.

## DeFi / Crypto

**TVL (Total Value Locked)** — USD value of assets deposited into a DeFi protocol. A rough proxy for protocol usage and health.

**DeFi (Decentralised Finance)** — Financial services (lending, trading, derivatives) built on public blockchains without centralised intermediaries.

**On-chain data** — Data read directly from a blockchain's public ledger: wallet flows, exchange balances, miner activity, etc.

**Halving** — Bitcoin event approximately every four years where the block subsidy is cut in half, reducing new supply issuance.

**L2 (Layer 2)** — Scaling solution built on top of a base blockchain (L1) to increase throughput and reduce fees (e.g., Arbitrum, Base on Ethereum).
